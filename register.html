What information we collect

Your name, parents' names, phone number, email address, gender, date of birth, address, blood group, department, courses, and photo.

Why we collect it

Only to process your registration and to contact you about it. It will not be used for any other purpose.

Who receives it

Only the website owner. Your form is sent over a secure (HTTPS) connection to the owner's email through a form service called FormSubmit. Your information will not be sold, and will not be shared with others unless required by law.

How long we keep it

Only as long as needed for your registration. After that, it will be deleted.

Sensitive information

Health-related details, such as your blood group, are considered sensitive personal information under the law. Please provide them only if needed.

Your rights as a data subject (Section 16, RA 10173)

  • To be informed about how your data is used
  • To access your data
  • To object to its processing
  • To correct wrong or outdated data
  • To request erasure or blocking of your data
  • To data portability (get a copy of your data)
  • To claim damages and file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission

To access, correct, or delete your information, email fsumandar.3903@umak.edu.ph.

What the law says

The Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), approved on August 15, 2012, protects the right to privacy of every individual while allowing the free flow of information.

  • Section 11: personal information must be handled with transparency (you are told what is collected and why), legitimate purpose (used only for the stated purpose), and proportionality (only what is needed is collected).
  • Sections 12 and 13: processing of personal and sensitive personal information generally needs your consent.
  • Section 20: those who handle personal information must use reasonable organizational, physical, and technical measures to protect it.

The law is enforced by the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).
